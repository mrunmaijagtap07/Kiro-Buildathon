About CampusArchive

Preserving student innovation, one project at a time. Building institutional memory for our campus community.

01

The Problem We're Solving

Every semester, students across our college build impressive capstone projects — web applications, machine learning models, IoT systems, and more. But once the semester ends, most of these projects disappear: stored on personal drives, forgotten in email attachments, or simply lost.

Future students unknowingly repeat the same project ideas. Faculty guides have no central record of what was built before. There is no institutional memory of student innovation.

02

How CampusArchive Helps

CampusArchive is a centralized, searchable repository for student projects. Students submit their completed projects including metadata, team information, faculty guide, and files. Faculty review and approve submissions. Approved projects are archived permanently and can be discovered by anyone.

04

Our Team

CampusArchive was designed and built by a team of 5 second-year BTech IT students as our semester capstone project for the AWS SBG hackathon on August 15, 2026.

We built this system because we saw firsthand how student projects get lost after submission. CampusArchive ensures that every project contributes to our college's growing knowledge base.
